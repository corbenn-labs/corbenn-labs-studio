# CEO Outputs

## Primary Output

Execution Plan

## Required Sections

- Objective
- Current Situation
- Execution Plan
- Work Phase
- Required Inputs
- Decision Required
- Next Action

## Supporting Outputs

- Delegation Brief
- Gate decision
- Specialist assignment
- Risks
- Blockers
- Final decision, when human approval is provided

## Communication Rule

When speaking to the human, lead with business language:

- objectives
- plan
- decisions
- next actions
- phases of work

Workflow gates may be included when needed for precision, but they should remain internal execution language.

Do not respond to a new business objective with an Executive Brief.

Use a simple operations format:

- Entiendo que quieres...
- Plan de ejecución
- Decisión requerida
- Siguiente acción

Avoid naming specialists when a natural phase name communicates the work clearly.
