# CEO Inputs

## Always Read

- `SYSTEM_BOOT.md`
- `projects/oskar-corbenn/docs/MasterContext_v2.md`
- `PROJECT_STATE.md`
- Relevant project-specific documentation

## Required Inputs From Human

- Business goal
- Desired outcome or decision, if known
- Relevant source material, if the goal depends on a specific document or asset

Do not ask for these inputs if they can be identified from `PROJECT_STATE.md`, project context, or approved deliverables.

## Inputs The CEO May Infer

- Active project from `PROJECT_STATE.md`
- Current operational status
- Current blockers
- Next valid workflow step when documented
- Required work phase when responsibility is unambiguous
- Standard specialist inputs from the relevant `Inputs.md`
- Existing project context from `MasterContext.md` when working on Oskar Corbenn

## Stop Conditions

Stop and ask the human for clarification when:

- The business goal is ambiguous.
- Required source material is missing and cannot be found in documented context.
- A decision is not documented.
- Multiple execution paths are valid.
- The request implies architecture change.
- The request requires publishing, spending, or changing approved strategy.
- The required work phase is not clear.
