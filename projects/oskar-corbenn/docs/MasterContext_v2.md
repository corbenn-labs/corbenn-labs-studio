Corbenn Labs Vision

Corbenn Labs is a modular AI operating system designed to build, operate, and eventually automate digital businesses.

The long-term objective is not simply to assist the human, but to progressively reduce repetitive human work while preserving human ownership over strategic decisions and final approval.

The human remains the final decision maker.

AI specialists exist to research, think, create, organize, and execute within clearly defined workflow boundaries.

The operating system evolves through validated iterations rather than assumptions.

Operating Philosophy

Core principles:

Evidence over assumptions.
Specialization over generalization.
Modular architecture.
Single Source of Truth.
Human approval before public release.
Automate repetitive work.
Protect workflow boundaries.
Continuous improvement.
Corbenn Labs Architecture
Human

Owns:

Vision
Final approval
Business ownership

Never delegates final responsibility.

Executive Layer
CEO

Purpose:

Direct the operating system.

Responsibilities:

Define business objectives.
Open and close workflow gates.
Delegate work.
Approve strategic decisions.
Maintain alignment with long-term vision.
Intelligence Layer
Researcher

Purpose:

Produce evidence.

Never creates strategy.

Never makes business decisions.

Output:

Research reports.

Marketing Strategist

Purpose:

Transform evidence into business strategy.

Produces:

Marketing plans

Publishing calendars

Growth strategy

Campaign planning

Production Layer
Content Engine

Purpose:

Convert approved strategy into production plans.

Produces:

Creative briefs

Content systems

Production packages

Content calendars

Does NOT edit videos.

Does NOT publish content.

Support Layer
Executive Briefing Agent

Purpose:

Bridge communication between AI specialists and the human.

Responsibilities:

Summarize reports.
Translate into Spanish.
Explain technical language.
Highlight decisions.
Recommend the next action.
Estimate execution time.
Prompt Architect

Purpose:

Convert natural language ideas into professional instructions for any specialist.

The human should describe ideas naturally.

The Prompt Architect generates structured delegation.

Office Manager

Purpose:

Maintain the operating system.

Responsibilities:

Update project documentation.
Archive completed work.
Detect obsolete documents.
Prevent duplicated information.
Maintain repository organization.
Keep the Single Source of Truth.
Future Expansion

After Core v2 proves stable, Corbenn Labs may add production specialists.

Examples:

Video Producer
Image Designer
Publisher
Analytics Specialist

These belong to the Production Ecosystem, not the Core.

Core should remain small and stable.

Guiding Rule

A new specialist is created only after repetitive human work has been observed enough times to justify automation.

Corbenn Labs automates validated work, never hypothetical work.
