#### Verified distributor requirements — DistroKid (official)

Source: https://support.distrokid.com/hc/en-us/articles/50784709021971-How-to-Fill-Out-AI-Credits

At upload, DistroKid asks if any portion was AI-generated. Categories:
- **The lyrics** — AI wrote the words
- **The music** — AI composed the melody
- **All of the audio** — everything listener hears is AI-generated
- **Part of the audio** — some AI, some human

If "All of the audio," also asks whether artist name is human or AI persona. Credits updatable post-upload at `distrokid.com/credits`. Metadata transmitted via DDEX to downstream platforms.

**Mapping to LCM documented metadata (observation — operator must confirm at upload):**
- Lyrics: **likely NOT "AI wrote lyrics"** — documented as human-led; AI assisted iteratively
- Music: **likely "The music" and/or "Part of the audio"** — MusicGPT generated instrumental
- Voice: **"Part of the audio" or component of AI-generated audio** — AI voice not human
- Recommended approach: **"Part of the audio"** with human lyrics + AI music + AI vocals — **pending operator verification** at upload UI

| Platform | Requirement | Source |

| **Spotify policy** | Prohibits impersonation/deepfakes; DDEX disclosure not penalized per Spotify Newsroom Sep 2025 / Apr 2026 beta | https://newsroom.spotify.com/2025-09-25/spotify-strengthens-ai-protections/ |
