# Campaign Objective

Launch *La Casa Más Chica* as the next operational single release within the *Razones Para Quedarme* album era — helping new and returning listeners discover Oskar Corbenn through a song centered on emotional distance, change, and the limits of external solutions.

This campaign is not intended to create a short-lived viral moment. Its purpose is to introduce or deepen audience trust in the emotional world of Oskar Corbenn, convert existing discovery infrastructure from *El Cambio de Tono* into durable audience relationships, and produce a repeatable release workflow that reduces operator workload without compromising Brand DNA.

At the same time, the campaign should contribute to building a sustainable music business capable of generating long-term revenue without compromising the artistic identity defined in Brand DNA.

This is operational work. It must not become a Core experiment, VOC cycle, or architecture expansion.

---

# Primary Business Goal

Increase the visibility and discoverability of Oskar Corbenn through the pre-release and release of *La Casa Más Chica*, while strengthening long-term audience growth infrastructure established during *El Cambio de Tono* and laying groundwork for subsequent *Razones Para Quedarme* chapters.

# Audience and Problem Context

## Target Audience

Spanish-speaking adults who appreciate emotionally honest storytelling, reflective lyrics, and cinematic rock music.

People who have experienced love, relational change, uncertainty, emotional distance while sharing a life or space with someone, personal growth, or emotional reconstruction.

## Problem Context

*La Casa Más Chica* addresses a specific emotional pattern: the hope that changing external circumstances — a new home, a smaller space, a new address — can repair what has deteriorated internally in a relationship.

The song speaks to listeners who recognize that some wounds, silences, and distances travel with us. The marketing problem is not to exploit personal pain, but to help the right audience find a song that mirrors a common human experience with introspection and dignity.

## Operator Artistic Priority (Non-Evidence)

The operator identifies *La Casa Más Chica* as a personally meaningful completed song. This context may inform internal prioritization but must **not** be treated as evidence of audience demand, platform priority, market potential, or expected commercial performance.

All campaign work must preserve established Oskar Corbenn narrative principles:

* Never identify or name private individuals.
* Never publicly assign blame, humiliate, attack, expose, or target anyone.
* Never frame Oskar Corbenn as a victim.
* Never use pain as a weapon to obtain sympathy.
* Preserve dignity for all people involved.
* Favor introspection over accusation.

# Open Questions for Researcher

The G2 Research Report must address the following questions for *La Casa Más Chica* specifically. Prior ECT or album-cadence findings may be cited as context but do not replace release-specific answers.

1. **Discoverability baseline:** Given current documented presence state (`OfficialPresence.md`, `PROJECT_STATE.md`, and approved presence optimization strategy), how discoverable is Oskar Corbenn today entering the *La Casa Más Chica* pre-release window?

2. **Platform opportunity:** Which platforms currently offer the greatest evidence-supported opportunity for this release — considering the song's themes (emotional distance, home/moving symbolism, introspective relational pain) and the verified *El Cambio de Tono* performance patterns?

3. **Conversion barriers:** What are the biggest evidence-supported barriers preventing existing *El Cambio de Tono* discovery signals from converting into sustained audience readiness for the next single?

4. **Content format priority:** Which content formats (long-form anchor, story-hook Shorts, lyric-led clips, visual motifs such as rain/houses/roads) are best supported by verified project evidence for a pre-release and release campaign for this song — without contradicting Brand DNA?

5. **Readiness alignment:** What is the current documented status of the Pre-Release Readiness Checklist Tier A and Tier B criteria relative to the operator target date **2026-08-07**? Which gaps most constrain a responsible pre-release marketing start?

6. **Calendar feasibility:** Given the conditionally adopted album rollout framework, documented blockers, Corbenn Labs single-operator capacity, and the operator target date **2026-08-07** *(not guaranteed feasible)* — what does evidence support regarding whether the target date is operationally sound? If not, what constraints drive that assessment?

7. **Comparable discovery models:** Which documented comparable artists or campaign patterns successfully reach a similar audience with story-led, introspective rock — and what transferable patterns apply without trend-chasing?

8. **Distribution and disclosure:** What verified distributor and platform requirements apply to AI-generated music/voice disclosure for this release, given documented metadata — and what remains unknown pending operator verification?
