## Campaign Objective

Launch *La Casa Más Chica* as **Chapter 2** of *Razones Para Quedarme*, converting existing discovery infrastructure from *El Cambio de Tono* into durable audience relationships for a song about emotional distance and the limits of external change — without pursuing short-lived virality or sacrificing Brand DNA.

**Primary business goal (from Campaign Brief):** Increase visibility and discoverability through *La Casa Más Chica* while strengthening long-term audience growth infrastructure and reducing operator workload versus prior release cycles.

**Strategic framing:** *La Casa Más Chica* is the second operational chapter of the album era — not an isolated disposable single. Every initiative must benefit future *Razones Para Quedarme* releases.

## Primary Audience

**Core segment:** Spanish-speaking adults who appreciate emotionally honest storytelling, reflective lyrics, and cinematic rock music.

**Psychographic profile:** People who have experienced love, relational change, uncertainty, **emotional distance while sharing a life or space with someone**, personal growth, or emotional reconstruction. They respond to introspection and dignity — not accusation or sensationalism.

**LCM-specific resonance:** Listeners who recognize that changing houses, addresses, or routines does not automatically repair internal wounds. The song's central metaphor — *"Pero el silencio hizo el viaje sentado entre los dos"* — targets this recognition without exploiting personal pain.

**Discovery behavior:** Passive encounter (YouTube Shorts feed, DSP browse, title search) before active followership (subscribe, save, return). Trust threshold: willing to invest ~5 minutes when artist identity is clear and surfaces feel official.

**Not the primary audience:** Trend-chasing Gen Z TikTok natives seeking meme-native formats. Pursuing this segment contradicts Brand DNA and campaign constraints.

**Operator artistic priority (non-evidence):** The operator's personal connection to *La Casa Más Chica* may inform internal prioritization but must **not** be treated as audience demand, platform priority, or performance prediction.
